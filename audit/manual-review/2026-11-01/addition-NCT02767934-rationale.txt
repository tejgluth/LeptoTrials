Registry exclusion and posted protocol section 5.1.3(8), pages 11–12, exclude active CNS leukemia/leukemic meningitis but explicitly permit previously treated stable CNS leukemia with no CSF blasts. This supports a historical, cleared leukemic-meningitis eligibility path, consistent with the treated CNS-lymphoma exceptions already included. ALL minimal residual marrow disease remains required; active meningitis is not eligible. Terminated status is retained and hidden by default recruitment filters. No solid-tumor bucket applies.
