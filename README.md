# LeptoTrials

Find and explore clinical trials relevant to leptomeningeal metastasis.

**[Visit LeptoTrials → leptotrials.com](https://leptotrials.com)**

LeptoTrials helps patients and caregivers navigate clinical trial options for leptomeningeal disease. It brings study details, eligibility criteria, locations, and available trial contacts together to help users prepare for a conversation with their care team.

## What you can do

- Search by age, primary tumor type, country or continent, study type, phase, and recruitment status.
- Read trial summaries and eligibility criteria in one place.
- Find study locations and available contacts.
- Prepare an editable email inquiry to a trial team.
- Open the original ClinicalTrials.gov record for further details.
- Browse without creating an account.
- Suggest a trial for review using its official ClinicalTrials.gov link.

## Using LeptoTrials

1. Visit [leptotrials.com](https://leptotrials.com) and select the filters relevant to your search.
2. Browse the matching studies and review their recruitment status, locations, and eligibility criteria.
3. Discuss potential options with your care team and contact the study team to confirm availability and eligibility.

You can leave filters open to explore broadly or refine them to narrow the results. A study appearing in the results does not mean that enrollment is available or that a particular patient qualifies.

## How studies are selected

LeptoTrials searches ClinicalTrials.gov for leptomeningeal-related studies, combines overlapping results, and applies the selected filters. Screening considers study descriptions and eligibility text, supported by manually reviewed classifications.

Trial records and enrollment requirements can change. LeptoTrials is a discovery tool, not a clinical eligibility assessment or a substitute for medical advice. The default results include **active, not recruiting** studies. Always confirm details with the study team and your clinician.

## Data source

Trial information comes from [ClinicalTrials.gov](https://clinicaltrials.gov). LeptoTrials is an independent application.

## Contact analytics

The app records two anonymous contact-intent events: clicking a trial contact's email link and copying a trial contact's email address.
